# Source exclusion register

Read this register before researching or proposing a previously reviewed source. The owner requires free access and free direct employer applications. Do not revisit excluded candidates unless the owner explicitly asks. This records decisions in the repository so they persist across development sessions.

| Source | Excluded on | Reason | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jobr, jobr.pro | October 20, 2026 | The free feed sends applicants through a paid-service/free-manual-application choice. Automatic direct-employer resolution failed. The owner explicitly rejected paid-service involvement. | Excluded. No subscription, account, adapter or active source. Do not propose it again. [Evidence](JOBR_PILOT_VALIDATION.md). |

The requirement concerns the integration and application path. Existing approved free infrastructure does not become paid usage simply because its provider sells other products. Never register for a paid plan, trial or billing account as a fallback.
