# Source exclusion register

Read this register before researching or proposing a previously reviewed source. The owner requires free access and free direct employer applications. Do not revisit excluded candidates unless the owner explicitly asks. This records decisions in the repository so they persist across development sessions.

| Source | Excluded on | Reason | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jobr, jobr.pro | October 20, 2026 | The free feed sends applicants through a paid-service/free-manual-application choice. Automatic direct-employer resolution failed. The owner explicitly rejected paid-service involvement. | Excluded. No subscription, account, adapter or active source. Do not propose it again. [Evidence](JOBR_PILOT_VALIDATION.md). |
| [Zapply Internships](https://github.com/zapplyjobs/Internships-2027) | October 27, 2026 | Repository terms prohibit resubmitting listings to third-party aggregators or feeds. Its links led through Zapply in the October 18 audit. | Do not integrate without a reviewed change in terms or permission for this use. [Audit](SOURCE_EXPANSION_AUDIT.md); [terms](https://github.com/zapplyjobs/Internships-2027/blob/main/LICENSE). |
| [HiringCafe](https://hiringcafe.com/) | October 27, 2026 | Published site terms restrict copying, republishing and redistributing site material. | Do not integrate without an authorized arrangement or reviewed change in terms. [Terms](https://hiringcafe.com/terms-and-conditions). |
| [Jobbie internships](https://github.com/jobbie-bot/Summer-2027-Tech-Internships) | October 27, 2026 | Preliminary source-only screen against the current production snapshot found two apparently unmatched candidates passing local checks; employer data confirmed a location mismatch in one. Full refresh and net-new yield were not verified. | Not integrated. Reconsider only on owner request, with current yield and employer location verification. [Review](JOBBIE_SOURCE_REVIEW.md). |

The requirement concerns the integration and application path. Existing approved free infrastructure does not become paid usage simply because its provider sells other products. Never register for a paid plan, trial or billing account as a fallback.
