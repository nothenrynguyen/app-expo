# Jobright comparison boundary

Reviewed October 29, 2026 (Pacific). The owner identified Jobright as the board behind the larger-count comparison. No account, payment method, feed, hidden endpoint, automated pagination, listing corpus or integration was created.

## Finding

Jobright's [homepage](https://jobright.ai/) advertises more than eight million total jobs. This is a broad platform claim, not a verified count of open internships or new-graduate roles matching empluzzed's countries and categories. Public keyword-search counts and capped headings are not interchangeable with the platform-wide inventory or a logged-in filtered result set. The reported 10k+ per student collection has not been independently validated.

The [terms of service](https://jobright.ai/legal/service), last updated July 29, 2026 on the inspected page, restrict systematic collection without written permission, automated extraction, redistribution of job/employer data and use of the service to compete. They do not provide clearance for the proposed automated 100-internship/100-new-grad competitor dataset. A free/noncommercial site is not assumed exempt from those restrictions.

The automated Jobright benchmark is therefore on hold. Do not copy its postings, decode its application routes, consume a mirror to bypass restrictions, or use it as an unapproved source. This is a conservative operational reading of published terms, not a legal determination. No permission request or other provider message was sent.

## Work that can continue

Use empluzzed's own historical/current snapshots, approved licensed inputs and independently reviewed employer sources to measure discovery and verification losses. Do not imply this independently collected sample is a completed Jobright coverage benchmark. A representative direct Jobright comparison requires an authorized route or a separately reviewed permission change.

The substantive internal diagnosis is in [coverage loss diagnosis](COVERAGE_LOSS_DIAGNOSIS.md). It identifies missing employer evidence and missing permitted replacement inputs as the dominant causes, rather than assuming competing boards contain only bad jobs.
