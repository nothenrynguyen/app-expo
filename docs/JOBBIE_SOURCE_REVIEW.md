# Jobbie source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. Research only. No Jobbie feed is active or published.

The repository README carried 223 postings; the trial parser read 217. Against the production snapshot generated October 27 at 08:20 UTC and the then-current repository company registry and listing health data, the preliminary source-only screen held 125 for company review, flagged 68 for unsupported roles, 20 for location, and two for freshness. Two candidates passed as apparently unmatched. These outcomes are ordered screening categories, not a count of unique new jobs; no full refresh or definitive net yield was completed.

One apparently unmatched RTX software internship was listed by Jobbie in San Antonio, Texas, while the employer Workday detail identified San Diego, California. The other Workday detail was accessible although its board name contained Private_Posting; that name alone does not establish that the listing is private. Both passed the simple local screen, not a full publication review.

Decision: do not integrate for now. The trial did not establish sufficient reliable incremental value, and the confirmed location discrepancy requires an employer-verification approach. The experimental local parser, configuration and license notice were removed. Reconsider on owner request using a fresh snapshot, full refresh, employer verification, and a new source and license review.
