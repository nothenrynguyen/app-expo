## Proposed next work: coverage and eligibility audit

1. Freeze a current snapshot and reconcile unique opportunities by collection,
   employer, role family and source. Separate source credits from unique jobs.
2. Review a bounded, diverse sample of current early-career postings and permitted
   official employer boards. Compare published jobs with eligible jobs missed by
   discovery, title rules, employer evidence, availability holds or deduplication.
3. Assess required versus preferred experience, degree alternatives and generic
   titles. Keep uncertain cases separate. Do not assume Engineer II is ineligible.
4. Produce examples and measured keep/add/exclude/uncertain counts for candidate
   policies, including false exclusions. State sample limits rather than claiming
   market-wide recall or a target internship/full-time ratio.
5. Recommend one focused implementation with a frozen before/after comparison,
   regression cases and explicit approval before broader exclusions or new feeds.

The audit is proposed, not implemented by this label release. Prioritize fixing
missed eligible opportunities if demonstrated. If experience evidence remains
ambiguous, prefer clearer labels or review holds over blanket exclusions. Preserve
zero-cost operation and source permissions. No eligibility ceiling is chosen yet.
